# Christopher Hunt — The House Edge

## Register

brand

## Users & Purpose

**Primary audience:** Hiring executives, design leaders, and product partners in regulated gambling and adjacent AI CX — people who decide whether to bring Christopher in for director-level work.

**Job to be done:** In under 90 seconds, understand who Christopher is, see proof of craft at scale, and feel confident enough to reach out.

**Emotional target:** Controlled confidence. Premium operator energy — not casino costume, not SaaS template. The site should feel authored by someone who ships billion-tap products.

## Brand Personality

Precise · Commanding · Insider

Christopher knows the floor. The portfolio speaks like a creative director who has lived inside sportsbooks and control rooms, not someone who read a brief about gambling.

## Anti-References

- Generic dark-mode AI portfolio (mint accent, glass cards, uppercase eyebrows)
- Shadcn template landing pages
- Literal casino clipart or slot-machine aesthetics
- Dashboard-card grids for everything
- "Experience points" gamification copy
- Fraunces + mono labels + ruled separators editorial lane

## Strategic Design Principles

1. **Typography carries the brand.** Layout supports type; type does not decorate layout.
2. **One interactive moment.** The Spiffing blackjack table is the signature beat — everything else is editorial proof.
3. **Gambling as subtext.** Metaphors (hands, lines, house edge) appear in copy and pacing, not as UI costumes.
4. **Show director-level decisions.** Case studies lead with outcomes and constraints, not feature lists.
5. **Motion with intent.** Reveals serve narrative; no fade-on-scroll for every block.

## Design System

Visual language inspired by [Finach](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/finach) — warm minimal canvas, mega typographic hero (IAM / CHRIS), Finach-style work index with hover preview, horizontal gallery scroll, inverted experience band with giant year numbers. Site brand: **IAM CHRIS**.
